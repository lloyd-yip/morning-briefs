Who he is

  • Bruce is Brand Strategist, Chief Creative Officer, and Managing Partner at Echo Brand Group, where he has been since October 2012. The firm is based in Wilton, Connecticut (his LinkedIn).
  • He has about 30 years in brand marketing. From 1987 to 1999 he was VP of Advertising and Brand Communications at the NFL, where he built the league's first in-house brand marketing division (echobrandgroup.com About page).
  • Before Echo he also served as President of Oneworld Communications and Chief Marketing Officer at Adina Beverages (his LinkedIn).

The business

  • Echo Brand Group sells brand strategy, creative design, web development, advertising, video production, and marketing automation, on a project, advisory, or retainer basis (echobrandgroup.com).
  • Their tagline is "We make brands echo." Named work includes the NFL, NBA, NHL, US Open tennis, PepsiCo, Zebra Technologies, and Azelis (echobrandgroup.com, his LinkedIn).
  • The site shows no pricing, no lead magnet, and no demo request or newsletter signup, only a contact form and a blog (echobrandgroup.com).

Who they sell to

  • Marketing leaders and brand owners at sports organizations, consumer brands, and mid-size companies that want a scalable team of digital experts without hiring in-house (echobrandgroup.com).

Problem they solve

  • Companies that need strong brand and advertising work but do not want to build a full creative team. Echo gives them senior strategy and execution when they need it (echobrandgroup.com).

What’s interesting for this call

  • His own credentials are very strong, but the public site shows no repeatable way to bring in new clients, other than a contact form. Ask how new Echo clients arrive today and how much of it is referral (echobrandgroup.com).
  • Much of his proof is big brand work from earlier years. Ask what kind of client he most wants to add next.
  • He is a hands-on creative as well as a strategist. He wrote, directed, and produced the 2022 Telly Award winning NBA video promo "It's Who We Are" (his LinkedIn).

Rapport (personal)

  • He has directed TV spots himself, including NFL season kickoff and playoff commercials, and keeps them on a Vimeo page. Ask which spot he is proudest of (his Vimeo page, vimeo.com/bruceburketv).
  • He built the NFL's first in-house brand agency from scratch. Ask whether that was his idea or the league came to him with it (echobrandgroup.com About page).
