Who he is

  • Thomas "Tom" Marcin is a co-founder of Evolution Cloud Services, which goes by EVOCS. He is based in the Greater Philadelphia area.
  • The EVOCS team page lists him as Co-Founder, Chief Delivery Officer, and CEO of EVOCS India. It says he has more than 30 years of international experience and leads the India team.
  • His LinkedIn shows earlier roles as Vice President of Service Delivery at EVOCS, management consulting at West Monroe, and Vice President of Engineering and Operations at MasTec Professional Services.
  • His LinkedIn headline now says CEO of Velloe since May 2026. Velloe is based in Singapore and offers technology advisory and AI data center design, construction, and operations for the Asia-Pacific region. Ask early which company this call is about.

The business

  • EVOCS is a boutique technology consultancy. Its homepage says it is a team of senior practitioners and "not a staffing farm or a generalist shop."
  • It does four things. It gives advisory help on strategy, procurement, HR, and mergers. It rolls out big business software like Workday, HiBob, Oracle, Salesforce, and ServiceNow. It builds data reporting. It also builds custom software and AI tools.
  • It also sells ongoing managed support for Workday after go-live, including fixes, upgrades, reporting, and training.
  • The homepage claims a 91% client retention rate, a Net Promoter Score of 86, a 15% average cut in implementation costs, and more than 500 projects. LinkedIn lists the company at about 60 to 70 people, spread across the United States, India, Argentina, and Vietnam.

Who they sell to

  • Mid-size and large organizations going through a big system change, like moving HR or finance onto Workday.
  • The site says it focuses on regulated and high-stakes environments.
  • The buyer is usually a CHRO, CIO, CFO, or HR technology leader who owns the software rollout.

Problem they solve

  • Big software rollouts often run late, cost too much, and leave the internal team stuck with problems after go-live.
  • EVOCS helps companies pick the right system, install it, and keep it running, with the same team staying accountable after launch.
  • For Workday customers, it takes over the twice-a-year update cycle so the internal team is not buried in testing.
