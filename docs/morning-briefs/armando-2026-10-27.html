Who he is

  • Michael (he goes by Mike on the company site) is President and CEO of Pinnacle Health Group, also called PHG. He sets the strategy, runs operations, and directs marketing and sales himself.
  • He co-founded PHG in 1994 with his late business partner, John R. Couvillon, so he has run this company for over 30 years.
  • He was on the board of the National Association of Physician Recruiters for more than 10 years and was its President in 2004–2005. He also helped build the "Physician Placement Starting Salary Survey" with that group and the MGMA (the Medical Group Management Association).
  • He has an MBA from Xavier University and a chemistry degree from Miami University, both in Ohio.

The business

  • PHG is a physician recruiting firm headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, with associate offices in Florida, Missouri, and Ohio.
  • They sell permanent placement of physicians, advanced practice providers (like nurse practitioners and PAs) and physician executives. They also run a locum tenens division, which places temporary doctors to cover gaps.
  • Their site says they have made "thousands of successful placements" and calls their approach a "40 Year Proven Method." Pricing is quote-only, and they stress no hourly billing and no escalating costs.
  • They say 70% of their business comes from repeat customers.
  • The leadership page lists a VP of Business Development, a VP of Locum Tenens, a VP of Recruitment, and a VP of Communications, so Mike has a real team under him and a dedicated sales lead.

Who they sell to

  • Hospitals and health systems.
  • Medical groups, academic medical centers, and FQHCs (federally qualified health centers, which are community clinics).
  • On the other side, they also court physicians and providers who are looking for a new job, through job alerts, CV uploads and featured openings.

Problem they solve

  • Communities and hospitals that cannot fill doctor and provider jobs lose patient access while the role sits empty.
  • Physician searches are slow and hard, so employers want a firm that understands their culture and clinical needs and keeps them updated the whole way.
  • Short-term gaps need fast cover, which is where locum tenens comes in.

What’s interesting for this call

  • He came in through a quantumSCALE webinar and booked this Roadmap call himself, so he has already seen how we think.
  • With 70% repeat business, new client growth is the open question. Ask how PHG wins a brand-new hospital or health system today, and how many new client relationships they add in a year.
  • Their buyers are hospital leaders and physician practice heads, who are busy and hard to reach. That fits an educational webinar, for example on the physician shortage and what it costs a hospital when a role sits empty.
  • Mike is a marketing-minded CEO. He has publicly said physician recruiting is "a true executive search approach" with candidate-specific messages. Pitch precision and consulting depth, not volume or speed.
  • He already owns an asset that buyers want: the salary survey work. Ask whether PHG uses it today to start conversations with hospitals.
  • Ask early who else would weigh in on a growth decision, especially the VP of Business Development, Rob Followell.

Rapport (personal)

  • His company bio says he is an avid baseball fan and a little league coach, and "first and foremost, a family man." (PHG leadership bio on phg.com)
