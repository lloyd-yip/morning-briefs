Who she is

  • Lisa Thorson is the founder of Run The Numbers, which she started in February 2021. She is based in the Denver area.
  • She has more than 14 years in the consumer packaged goods (CPG) industry, mostly natural foods, and more than 8 years in data analytics.
  • She has worked on both sides of the table: as a broker placing brands into grocery stores, and as a national accounts sales manager at Laird Superfood, a brand that went public during 2020.
  • She also runs the Finance Forum for Naturally Colorado, a nonprofit network for Colorado food and beverage brands.

The business

  • Run The Numbers is a team of fractional consultants for food and beverage brands. Their headline is "Data Powering your CPG Growth."
  • They place experienced part-time consultants into sales, operations and deductions (money retailers and distributors take back from the brand) for brands doing $0 to $30M.
  • They also build data dashboards and reporting for brands doing $5M to $350M, and for agencies, brokers and consultants who serve brands.
  • They run an Emerging Brand Program that gives a new brand a ready-to-use retail strategy in two to three months.
  • It is a small, woman-owned firm with about 1 to 10 people. Their site lists Denver, Boston, DC and Phoenix.

Who they sell to

  • Founders of natural and better-for-you food and beverage brands selling into grocery retail, such as Whole Foods, Sprouts and Safeway.
  • Growing brands that need sales, operations or data help but are not ready to hire full-time.
  • Agencies, brokers and consultants who need data dashboards for their own brand clients.

Problem they solve

  • Many food brands get into big grocery chains and then find they are not making money because of slotting fees, promotions and deductions.
  • Founders often do not know their real margins, so they say yes to costly promotions or retailers they cannot afford.
  • Small brands need senior sales and operations help but cannot pay for full-time hires.

What’s interesting for this call

  • She already sells through education. She speaks on food industry podcasts, runs a finance forum, and offers free margin and projection calculators on her site. A webinar is a natural next step for her, not a new habit.
  • Her market is a clear niche: food and beverage founders going into grocery retail. Ask how many new brands she signs a month today and how they find her.
  • Ask how much of her business comes from referrals inside the Naturally Colorado network, and whether she wants to reach brands outside Colorado.
