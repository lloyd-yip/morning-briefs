Larry Warren · Leadership International · Call 1 · Fri Oct 9, 2026 · 5:00 PM ET · Edwin

Who he is

  • Larry Warren is the Founder and President of Leadership International.
  • He is based in Brentwood, Tennessee, just outside Nashville.
  • He has led Leadership International since 2010.
  • Before that, he founded African Leadership and served as its president for about ten years.

The business

  • Leadership International is a Christian nonprofit that runs classroom training centers for church leaders and pastors.
  • With those local leaders, it also funds relief and service projects in the same communities, including schooling for orphans and vulnerable children.
  • Its website says it serves in 36 countries, has run 329 training classes, and has about 3,500 current students.
  • Its main offices are in Brentwood, Tennessee, and Nairobi, Kenya.

Who they sell to

  • The people they serve are pastors and church leaders, mostly in Africa and South Asia, who take the training.
  • The people who pay for the work are donors, churches, and foundations in the United States who fund the training and the relief projects.

Problem they solve

  • Many pastors in these regions lead large churches with little or no formal Bible or leadership training.
  • Leadership International gives them structured training they can use in their churches and pass on to other leaders.

What’s interesting for this call

  • This is a nonprofit, so growth likely means more donors and more funding, not more customers. Ask early who he wants to reach and what a new donor or partner is worth to him.

  • He was auto-registered for the QS webinar and then booked this call himself after a reminder, so find out what made him book.
