Seth Maust · Five Star Life · Call 1 · Wed Oct 28, 2026, 3:15 PM ET · Ryan

Who he is

  • Seth is the co-founder and President of Five Star Life, a nonprofit based in Elkhart, Indiana. He has led it since 2005.
  • He hosts a weekly podcast called "Five Star Life with Coach Seth," about mindset, leadership and parenting. On the podcast he goes by Coach Seth.
  • He has been on local TV (WNDU) talking about the organization and its basketball program.

The business

  • Five Star Life is a nonprofit with the mission "Transforming students' lives by challenging the way they think, act, and lead." It was started to help lower the high school dropout rate.
  • They sell a video curriculum for schools, experiential programs at their Summit campus, after-school clubs, professional development, and a parent mobile app.
  • They also run Five Star Life Basketball, a youth training academy with leagues, AAU teams, camps and private training. It moved into the new Mishawaka Fieldhouse in fall 2024 after six years of bouncing between gyms.
  • They raise money through donations too, including a "Sponsor a Student for $25 a month" option.
  • Their site says up to 80% of schools see improved GPAs or keep a 3.0+, and up to 70% see fewer discipline referrals.

Who they sell to

  • Schools and school districts that buy the curriculum and programs.
  • Parents and families who sign kids up for basketball and programs.
  • Donors and sponsors who fund students.

Problem they solve

  • Many kids disengage from school and some drop out. Schools struggle with behavior, motivation and grades.
  • Five Star Life teaches kids a winning mindset so they show up, behave better and do better in class.

What’s interesting for this call

  • He came in from a quantumSCALE webinar and booked from the webinar chat, so he has heard how we think.
  • This is a nonprofit with several buyer types at once. Ask which one he wants to grow first: more schools buying the curriculum, more families in programs, or more donors. A webinar works very differently for each.
  • School leaders and principals respond well to education, so a webinar on something like "how schools cut discipline referrals and raise GPAs" fits his strongest proof.
  • He already runs a podcast, so he is comfortable on camera and has content. Ask how the podcast turns into new schools or donors today.
