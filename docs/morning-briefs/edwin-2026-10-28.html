Ronak · emPower eLearning · Call 1 · Wed Oct 28, 2026, 1:00 PM ET · Edwin

Who he is

  • Ronak works at emPower eLearning and booked this Roadmap call with his company email.
  • His exact title is not listed on the company site or in public profiles, so ask early what he owns. Growth, marketing, sales and partnerships are all possible.
  • The company is led by Hardik Parikh, Co-founder and CEO, and Chetan Parikh, Managing Director. If Ronak is not one of the deciders, find out how growth decisions get made with them.

The business

  • emPower sells AI-powered compliance training software for healthcare and food service teams. Their line is "AI-powered compliance training for healthcare and food teams."
  • The platform covers 100+ ready-made courses (like HIPAA, OSHA bloodborne pathogens and Joint Commission), AI that turns policies into training, policy management, and audit-ready reports.
  • They started in 2008 in Louisville, Kentucky. Their site says they serve 1,000+ organizations and 800,000+ learners, with a 50+ person team.
  • Pricing is public. It is free for up to 10 users, then about $2.50 per user per month for 11 to 250 users (with a $2,000 annual minimum), and custom pricing for enterprise.
  • Their About page says they grew "never with a big sales team. Just product that worked."

Who they sell to

  • Healthcare organizations, from 50-person clinics up to 50,000-employee health systems.
  • Food service and restaurant operators who need food safety and allergen training.
  • The person who buys is usually a compliance, HR or operations leader who has to prove training is done when an auditor shows up.

Problem they solve

  • Compliance training eats time. Someone has to build courses, chase staff to finish them, and pull reports together by hand in spreadsheets.
  • When an audit comes, missing records turn into real risk and fines.
  • emPower promises audit-ready in under 30 days, without hiring extra coordinators to manage the spreadsheet.

What’s interesting for this call

  • He came in from a quantumSCALE webinar and booked from the webinar chat, so he has already heard how we think.
  • Their own site admits growth has come from product, not a sales team. That opens a clean question: how do they win new health systems today, and what happens when word of mouth slows down?
  • Their buyers are compliance and HR leaders at clinics and health systems, who love education. A webinar on something like "how to be audit-ready for HIPAA and Joint Commission without spreadsheets" fits that buyer well.
  • They have a free plan for up to 10 users. Ask how many free users turn into paid accounts, because that tells you whether the gap is getting leads in or turning them into customers.
