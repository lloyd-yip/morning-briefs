  • Ronit Belson is the CEO and co-founder of Sawmills.ai. Her LinkedIn puts her in Los Altos, California, and she has run the company since March 2024.

The business

  • Sawmills sells software that cleans up the flood of system data (logs, metrics, and traces) that engineering teams send to monitoring tools like Datadog. Its tagline is "Agentic telemetry platform. Your data, perfectly in tune."

  • Its LinkedIn company page shows about 10 to 20 employees across the US and Israel, about $10 million in total funding, and a 2024 founding date. Team8 is one of its investors.
  • The site publishes pricing, with plans starting at about $2,500 a month on an annual commitment, based on how much data a customer sends through.

Who they sell to

  • DevOps and platform engineering leaders at mid-size and large companies that pay big monitoring bills.
  • VPs of Engineering and CTOs who own the reliability of their systems and the budget for monitoring tools.
  • The named customer quotes on the site come from a Senior Director of DevOps and a VP of Engineering for platform and cloud infrastructure.

Problem they solve

  • Monitoring bills keep climbing because engineers ship code that creates tons of noisy data, while a separate DevOps team pays the bill. Ronit describes this mismatch on the Team8 podcast.
  • AI-written code makes it worse, because more code means more data.
  • Sawmills says one customer cut monitoring costs by 63% and cut the amount of data sent by 93%, according to the testimonial on its site.

Rapport (personal)

  • She and Erez worked together at Rollout.io and always said they wanted to build something together again. Sawmills is that second act. (Tech Optimist podcast, episode 96)
