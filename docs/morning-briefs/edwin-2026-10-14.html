Who they sell to

  • Memory care communities, nursing homes, and senior living operators.
  • Disability care organizations, therapists, and activity coordinators.
  • Some families who care for a loved one at home.

Problem they solve

  • People with advanced dementia or serious disabilities often become socially isolated because they can no longer talk with the people around them. Care staff are short on time, and families do not know what else to try.
  • Crdl gives staff and families a simple way to create real moments of contact and calm.
