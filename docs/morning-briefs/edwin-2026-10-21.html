The business

  • Favoland is an online beauty database. It rates beauty products and their ingredients for safety, skin fit, allergies, ethics, sustainability, and rules in each country.
  • It launched to the public in late 2024. The launch press release says it covered about 24,000 brands, 87,000 products, and 37,000 ingredients at that point.
  • Shoppers sign up for free and get personal ingredient advice. The plan is for brands to pay to claim their products, update their ingredient lists, and run surveys and ads to those shoppers.
  • The team is very small. LinkedIn and Dealroom list 2 to 10 people, with no outside funding on record. His launch post said the platform took three years and over a million dollars to build.

Who they sell to

  • Today the free side is everyday shoppers who want to know what is in their skincare and makeup.
  • The paying side is meant to be beauty brands and retailers who want to show they are honest about their ingredients and reach those shoppers.
  • He has posted on LinkedIn that he is looking for joint-venture partners to take Favoland into other countries, so partners may be a third buyer.

Problem they solve

  • Shoppers cannot easily tell what is in a beauty product, whether it is safe for their skin, or whether a brand's claims are true. Most of them end up guessing and wasting money on products that do not work for them.
  • Brands that are honest about ingredients have no easy way to prove it and stand out from brands that are not.
