Who he is

  • Collin Kramer is the Marketing Manager at C-Lock Inc. in Rapid City, South Dakota. He joined in about August 2025.
  • He sits on the sales and marketing team led by Brandon Peterson, the Director of Global Sales.
  • He writes columns for Beef Central, an Australian beef industry news site.

The business

  • C-Lock was founded in 2005 in Rapid City by Dr. Pat Zimmerman, a professor of atmospheric sciences. It builds and ships its own hardware from a factory in Rapid City.
  • Its main product is GreenFeed, a feeding station that measures methane and carbon dioxide from each animal while it eats. The second product line is SmartLine, which tracks feed intake, water intake, and weight for each animal.
  • C-Lock says GreenFeed data has been used in more than 800 peer-reviewed studies, and that it operates in more than 50 countries. It has sales advisors for the USA, Australia, Asia, and Europe, Latin America, and Africa.
  • Pricing is not on the site.

Who they sell to

  • Researchers at universities, government labs, and research stations who need trusted emissions data.
  • Livestock producers, such as feedlots, cow-calf operations, seedstock breeders, and dairies, who want to pick more efficient animals.
  • Large food and agriculture companies with sustainability goals.

Problem they solve

  • Two animals eating the same feed can perform very differently, and producers usually cannot see which one is wasting feed. C-Lock measures every animal so producers can make better breeding, feeding, and culling choices.
  • Researchers need emissions numbers that stand up to review, and GreenFeed gives them a standard method.
