Lead quality — important nuance

Blank lead quality on someone who never showed is not a rep failure. They never got far enough for scoring to matter.

The real problem is blank lead quality after someone showed to Call 1 (and especially after they moved forward). That means the closer didn’t enter it.

Insights / lessons

The live webinar still holds people. In-room retention is flat and healthy (~73% clear 10 minutes; serious watchers hang ~40 minutes).

What’s soft is attendance rate — fewer regs becoming live attendees. Booking rate among people who stayed 10+ is only slightly down (~12.3% → 11.2%), so the bigger leak is still getting the right people into the room.

Important: we didn’t change funnel mechanics or reminder sequences in a way that cleanly explains the show drop. The webinar content change seemed to help, not hurt. Root cause is still unclear (mix, traffic, seasonality, or swing).

What we’re doing anyway: rewrite show reminder sequences while we keep watching mix.

Wins

  • In-room retention is healthy. When people show, they stay. The live experience is still doing its job.
  • Booking rate among serious attendees is usable. About 11% of stayed-10+ people book in the last 30 days.
  • We know which segments show. SMB + training/IT behave; giant enterprise + weak-show finance labels mostly don’t at the webinar gate.

Failures

  • Attendance rate has been descending across recent webinars. That’s still the main top-of-funnel leak into live.
  • Lead quality entry after showed Call 1s is incomplete — especially Armando in August — so “qualified” still isn’t a clean scoreboard.

Strengths / gaps / risks / opportunities

  • Strengths: Sticky live experience; clear attendance-rate ICP pockets; measurable weekly funnel.
  • Gaps: No proven root cause for the attendance drop yet; incomplete LQ after showed calls; messy industry labels / missing contact data on ~1/3 of regs.
  • Risks: Spending more to acquire regs into a weak-attendance mix; debating lead quality without separating no-shows from showed-and-unscored.
  • Opportunities: Reminder rewrite; steer mix toward SMB 6–10 + training/IT; force LQ scoring after showed Call 1s.

Next

  3. Force lead-quality entry after Call-1 show (not for no-shows).

3) Tech (what matters)

Not restating Studio or the old stack. Everyone knows that exists.

A) SalesPulse — make it match the real sales process

Why: If the grader and scoreboards lag the live playbook, coaches coach the wrong thing and closers stop trusting the numbers.

What good looks like: grades follow current Call-1 / Call-2 frameworks; dates exist on graded calls for month-by-month behavior; DQ / lead-quality rules match how we actually qualify; freshness alerts still catch silent misses.

Impact: manager conversations become “your Call-1 collapsed here” instead of “maybe the score is weird.”

Current gap: thin dated samples and missing call dates on a lot of history — so the product exists, but the behavior scoreboard isn’t fully trustworthy yet.

B) Sales Wingmen — pre-call / post-call OS for each closer

Why: Revenue dies in the gaps — weak prep, weak notes, weak follow-up — more than in the webinar slide deck.

What we built toward: pre-call research packs, post-call notes by call type, follow-up drafts, morning briefs / open-loop nags.

Impact: each closer runs the same operating rhythm. Lloyd spends less time being the human integration layer.

Current gap: pays off only if SalesPulse + CRM fields (lead quality after showed calls, stages, titles) stay clean.

Insights

The leverage isn’t more dashboards. It’s SalesPulse reflecting the process we actually run, and Wingmen making that process unavoidable day to day.

Wins

  • Wingmen + briefs give closers a daily spine.
  • SalesPulse + watchdogs can catch stale coaching — when data is complete.

Failures

  • SalesPulse isn’t yet a clean month-by-month behavior mirror.
  • CRM blanks after showed Call 1s weaken both Pulse and Wingmen output.

Next

  1. SalesPulse alignment pass to current sales process.
  2. Enforce call dating + lead-quality completion after showed Call 1s.
  3. Keep Wingmen packs tied to that cleaned process.
